Click-to-refocus

Click anywhere on the image: the viewer picks the frame of the 9-frame focal stack that is sharpest at that spot. Idea and model from Tedla et al., “Learning to Refocus with Video Diffusion Models” (SIGGRAPH Asia 2025).

Make a stack from your own photo

Pick or take one photo. A cloud GPU runs the diffusion model and returns 9 focus positions (about 1–3 minutes). Your photo is sent to that server.

About this stack

Position 0 ≈ 5 cm … 8 ≈ infinity (per the authors' README; the exact spacing is not documented, so treat the order, not the distance, as reliable).

Sharpness is computed in your browser from the frames; stored stacks and uploads of 9 frames stay on your device; the optional photo generator sends your photo to a cloud GPU server. Credit: Tedla, Zhang, Zhang, Xin, Learning to Refocus with Video Diffusion Models, SIGGRAPH Asia 2025.
